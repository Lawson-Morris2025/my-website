Mountain & Coastal Drone Rules

Essential guidance for operating UAS in coastal zones, nature reserves, and National Parks.

1

Terrain Following (120m Rule)

When flying over steep mountain ridges or coastal cliffs, ensure your drone remains no more than 120 metres from the nearest ground point.

2

Harbour & Authority Checks

Check Cardiff Harbour Authority and local council bylaws before flying near barrages, piers, and populated public promenades.

3

Wildlife & SSSI Reserves

Respect nesting seabirds on cliffs (Nash Point, Sully Island) and waterfowl at nature reserves (Kenfig, Cosmeston).

4

Historic Monuments

Filming historic abbeys (Tintern, Neath) or castles (Caerphilly, Castell Coch) often requires landowner consent from Cadw.
