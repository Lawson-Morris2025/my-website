UK CAA Drone Code & Airspace Laws

Essential legal requirements for operating Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS) in UK airspace.

1

Visual Line of Sight (VLOS)

You must keep your drone in direct sight at all times without binoculars or goggles so you can see and avoid obstacles and other aircraft.

2

Maximum Altitude (400ft / 120m)

Never fly higher than 400 feet (120 metres) above the surface. On steep slopes or hills, maintain no more than 120m from the nearest point of the terrain.

3

Flight Restriction Zones (FRZs)

Strictly avoid restricted airspace around licensed airports and aerodromes unless you have coordinated digitally with air traffic control.

4

Landowner Permission

While CAA regulates airspace, you must obtain permission from the landowner (e.g., National Trust, Historic Scotland, Cadw, Forestry England) to take off and land.
